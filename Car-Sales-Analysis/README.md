# Car Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing car sales data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate sales performance, identify market trends, and generate meaningful business insights through an interactive dashboard.

The dataset contains 23,906 car sales records, including information about car manufacturers, models, body styles, colors, prices, dealers, and regions.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall car sales performance.
- Track sales trends over time.
- Compare sales across different car brands and body styles.
- Analyze sales distribution by vehicle color.
- Evaluate dealer and regional sales performance.
- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Data Preparation
- Reviewed the dataset structure and data quality.

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Sales
- Total Cars Sold
- Average Car Price
- Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales
- Year-over-Year (YOY) Sales Growth
- Cars Sold Growth

## Analysis
The dashboard explores:
- Monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Sales distribution by body style and car color.
- Sales performance across dealer regions.
- Average car prices and total vehicles sold.
- Year-to-date and year-over-year performance.

## Dashboard Features
The interactive dashboard includes:
- KPI cards to summarize sales performance.
- Charts to compare sales across vehicle categories.
- Trend analysis to monitor performance over time.
- Interactive slicers to filter and explore the data.

## Key Results
The analysis covers 23,906 car sales records with total sales of approximately $671.5 million. The dashboard enables users to explore sales patterns and compare performance across different categories and regions.
